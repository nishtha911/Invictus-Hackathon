StudyAbroad Edge Loan - GlobalEd Finance

Scheme Name: StudyAbroad Edge Loan
Bank: GlobalEd Finance

Overview:
Designed for students pursuing international degrees. Covers tuition, living allowance and visa-related expenses.

Eligibility:
- Students with confirmed admission to accredited foreign institutions.
- Minimum family income typically assessed; co-borrower preferred.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹3,00,000 to ₹5,000,000
- Interest rate: 11.0% p.a. (floating)
- Tenure: Up to 15 years
- Processing fee: 1.25%

Special Conditions:
- Partial disbursement linked to term start dates.
- Foreign currency conversion fees may apply; borrower responsible for forex costs.

Documentation: Offer letter, passport, visa documentation, fee schedule, co-borrower ID.
