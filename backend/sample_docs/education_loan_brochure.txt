About This Product

Invictus Bank's Education Loan is designed to help students achieve their academic
dreams without financial barriers. Whether you are pursuing engineering, medicine,
management, or any other professional course in India or abroad, we have a loan
product tailored for you.

================================================================================

Key Features

- Loan amount up to ₹20 lakh for studies in India
- Loan amount up to ₹40 lakh for studies abroad
- Competitive interest rates starting from 10.5% per annum
- Repayment holiday during the course (moratorium period)
- No collateral required for loans up to ₹4 lakh
- Special interest concession of 0.5% for female students
- Tax benefit under Section 80E of the Income Tax Act on interest paid

================================================================================

Eligible Courses

Undergraduate Courses:
- B.Tech / B.E. (Engineering)
- MBBS / BDS (Medical and Dental)
- B.Sc / B.Com / B.A.
- BBA / BCA
- Any UGC/AICTE approved undergraduate program

Postgraduate Courses:
- M.Tech / M.E.
- MBA / PGDM (from recognized institutions)
- M.Sc / M.Com / M.A.
- MD / MS (Medical)
- Any UGC/AICTE approved postgraduate program

Courses Abroad:
- Job-oriented professional and technical courses
- Courses offered by reputed universities listed in QS World Rankings
- MIM / MBA from top global business schools

================================================================================

Loan Amount and Margin

Studies in India:
- Up to ₹4 lakh: No margin required
- Above ₹4 lakh: 5% margin required from applicant

Studies Abroad:
- Up to ₹4 lakh: No margin required
- Above ₹4 lakh: 15% margin required from applicant

================================================================================

Interest Rates at a Glance

Loan Amount                        Interest Rate (p.a.)
Up to ₹4 lakh                      10.5% (floating)
₹4 lakh to ₹7.5 lakh              11.0% (floating)
Above ₹7.5 lakh                    11.5% (floating)
Female student concession          0.5% reduction on applicable rate

================================================================================

Repayment Period

- Loans up to ₹7.5 lakh: Up to 10 years after moratorium
- Loans above ₹7.5 lakh: Up to 15 years after moratorium

================================================================================

Tax Benefit

Interest paid on education loans is fully deductible under Section 80E of the
Income Tax Act for a period of 8 years from the year repayment begins.
There is no upper limit on the amount of interest that can be claimed.

================================================================================

How to Apply

1. Visit any Invictus Bank branch or apply online at www.invictusbank.com/loans
2. Submit the completed application form with required documents
3. Loan is processed within 7 working days of document submission
4. Sanction letter is issued and loan is disbursed directly to the institution
