BUSINESS LOAN — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
Invictus Bank — SME and Business Banking Division
Loan Category: Business Loan

================================================================================

Q1. Who is eligible for a business loan from Invictus Bank?
A. The following entities are eligible:
   - Sole proprietorships
   - Partnership firms
   - Private limited companies
   - Limited liability partnerships (LLPs)
   - Self-employed professionals (doctors, CAs, architects, etc.)
   The business must have been in operation for at least 3 years with a minimum
   annual turnover of ₹10 lakh and minimum net annual profit of ₹2 lakh.

================================================================================

Q2. What is the maximum loan amount available?
A. For unsecured business loans, the maximum is ₹50 lakh.
   For secured business loans (with collateral), the maximum is ₹5 crore.
   The loan amount is also subject to a maximum of 3 times the annual net profit.

================================================================================

Q3. What interest rates are charged on business loans?
A. Unsecured business loans:
   - CIBIL score 750 and above: 14.0% per annum
   - CIBIL score 700 to 749: 16.0% per annum
   Secured business loans:
   - CIBIL score 750 and above: 11.0% per annum
   - CIBIL score 700 to 749: 12.5% per annum
   Working capital (OD/CC): 13.5% per annum on utilized amount.

================================================================================

Q4. What is the maximum repayment tenure?
A. For unsecured business loans: Maximum 48 months (4 years).
   For secured term loans: Maximum 120 months (10 years).
   Working capital facilities are renewed annually.

================================================================================

Q5. Is collateral required for a business loan?
A. For unsecured business loans up to ₹50 lakh, no collateral is required.
   However, a personal guarantee from the proprietor/all partners/directors
   is mandatory.
   For secured loans above ₹50 lakh, immovable property with a minimum
   collateral coverage of 125% of the loan amount is required.

================================================================================

Q6. What is the minimum CIBIL score required?
A. The minimum CIBIL score required is 700 for the proprietor/partners/directors.

================================================================================

Q7. What documents are required?
A. Key documents include:
   - Business registration documents (as applicable to entity type)
   - Last 3 years Income Tax Returns (business and personal)
   - Last 3 years audited financial statements
   - Bank statements for last 12 months
   - GST returns for last 12 months
   - Identity and address proof of proprietor/partners/directors
   - Collateral documents (if applicable)

================================================================================

Q8. What is the processing fee?
A. For unsecured business loans: 2% of loan amount (minimum ₹2,000).
   For secured business loans: 1% of loan amount (minimum ₹5,000, maximum ₹50,000).
   For working capital facilities: 1% of sanctioned limit per annum.

================================================================================

Q9. Can I prepay my business loan?
A. Pre-payment of term loans is allowed after 12 months with a pre-payment
   charge of 2% on the outstanding principal.
   Foreclosure is allowed after 24 months without penalty for secured loans.

================================================================================

Q10. What can the business loan be used for?
A. Business loans can be used for:
   - Working capital requirements
   - Business expansion
   - Purchase of equipment or machinery
   - Renovation of business premises
   - Purchase of commercial property
   - Inventory financing
   Business loans cannot be used for personal expenses or speculative activities.

================================================================================

Q11. How long does approval take?
A. For unsecured business loans with complete documentation, approval typically
   takes 3 to 5 working days. Secured loans may take 7 to 10 working days due
   to property verification requirements.

================================================================================
