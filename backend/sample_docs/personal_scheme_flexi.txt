FlexiPersonal - UrbanCredit

Scheme Name: FlexiPersonal
Bank: UrbanCredit

Overview:
Flexible personal loan with EMI restructuring options and top-up facility.

Eligibility:
- Salaried or self-employed with 2 years track record.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹2,000,000
- Interest rate: From 11.5% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 7 years
- Processing fee: 1.0%

Special Conditions:
- Top-up available after 12 months of regular repayment.

Documentation: ID, income proof, bank statements.
