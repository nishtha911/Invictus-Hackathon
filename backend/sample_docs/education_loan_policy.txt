EDUCATION LOAN POLICY
Invictus Bank — Retail Lending Division
Document Type: Policy
Loan Category: Education Loan
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 01-April-2024

================================================================================

Overview

Invictus Bank offers education loans to support students pursuing higher education
in India and abroad. The bank is committed to making quality education accessible
by providing affordable financing options to eligible students and their families.

================================================================================

Eligibility

1. The applicant must be an Indian citizen.
2. The student must have secured admission to a recognized university or institution
   in India or abroad.
3. Minimum age of the student applicant: 18 years.
4. Maximum age of the student applicant: 35 years at the time of loan application.
5. A co-applicant (parent or guardian) is mandatory for all education loans.
6. The co-applicant must have a stable source of income.
7. Minimum co-applicant income: ₹25,000 per month.
8. The student must have passed the qualifying examination with a minimum of 50%
   aggregate marks.

================================================================================

Loan Amount

1. Minimum loan amount: ₹50,000
2. Maximum loan amount for studies in India: ₹20 lakh
3. Maximum loan amount for studies abroad: ₹40 lakh
4. Loans above ₹7.5 lakh require collateral security.
5. Loans up to ₹4 lakh require no margin contribution from the applicant.
6. For loans above ₹4 lakh for studies in India: margin of 5% required.
7. For loans above ₹4 lakh for studies abroad: margin of 15% required.

================================================================================

Interest Rate

1. For loans up to ₹4 lakh: 10.5% per annum (floating rate).
2. For loans above ₹4 lakh and up to ₹7.5 lakh: 11.0% per annum (floating rate).
3. For loans above ₹7.5 lakh: 11.5% per annum (floating rate).
4. Female students are eligible for a concession of 0.5% on the applicable rate.
5. Interest is charged on simple interest basis during the moratorium period.
6. After the moratorium period, interest is compounded monthly.

================================================================================

Tenure

1. Maximum repayment tenure for loans up to ₹7.5 lakh: 10 years.
2. Maximum repayment tenure for loans above ₹7.5 lakh: 15 years.
3. Repayment begins after the moratorium period ends.

================================================================================

Moratorium

1. The moratorium period is the course duration plus 12 months after course completion,
   or 6 months after getting a job, whichever is earlier.
2. During the moratorium period, only simple interest is charged.
3. Repayment of principal begins after the moratorium period.

================================================================================

Collateral

1. Loans up to ₹4 lakh: No collateral required. Parent/guardian as co-borrower is sufficient.
2. Loans above ₹4 lakh and up to ₹7.5 lakh: Suitable third-party guarantee required.
3. Loans above ₹7.5 lakh: Tangible collateral security of suitable value required.
   Acceptable collateral includes:
   - Immovable property (land or building)
   - Fixed deposits with Invictus Bank
   - Government securities
   - Life insurance policies with surrender value

================================================================================

Required Documents

The following documents must be submitted along with the loan application:

1. Completed loan application form
2. Passport-size photographs (2 copies)
3. Photo identity proof (Aadhaar card, PAN card, or passport)
4. Address proof (utility bill, Aadhaar card, or bank statement)
5. Admission letter from the institution
6. Fee structure / cost of education document from the institution
7. Mark sheets of last qualifying examination
8. Income proof of co-applicant:
   - Salaried: Last 3 months salary slips + Form 16
   - Self-employed: Last 2 years ITR with computation
9. Bank statements of co-applicant for last 6 months
10. Collateral documents (if applicable)
11. For studies abroad: Valid passport and visa (if available)

================================================================================

Processing Fee

1. Processing fee: 1% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹1,000.
2. Processing fee is non-refundable once the loan is sanctioned.
3. No processing fee for loans up to ₹4 lakh for students from economically
   weaker sections (EWS) with annual family income below ₹3 lakh.

================================================================================

Repayment

1. Repayment is through Equated Monthly Instalments (EMI).
2. EMI begins after the moratorium period.
3. Pre-payment is allowed without any penalty after 12 months of first disbursement.
4. Pre-payment penalty of 2% applies if loan is closed within 12 months of disbursement.
5. Repayment can be made through:
   - Auto-debit from Invictus Bank savings account
   - NACH mandate from any bank account
   - Online transfer / NEFT

================================================================================

Special Conditions

1. The loan is disbursed directly to the institution in instalments as per the
   fee schedule.
2. Living expenses, books, and equipment costs can be included in the loan amount
   subject to a maximum of 20% of the total loan.
3. Students who secure scholarships must inform the bank, and the loan amount
   will be adjusted accordingly.
4. The bank reserves the right to recall the loan if the student discontinues
   the course without prior intimation.
5. Insurance cover for the student borrower is mandatory for loans above ₹10 lakh.

================================================================================

Restrictions

1. The loan cannot be used for correspondence or distance learning courses
   unless the institution is recognized by UGC/AICTE.
2. Loans are not available for coaching classes or short-term certificate courses
   of less than 1 year duration.
3. The loan cannot be transferred to another student.

================================================================================
