PERSONAL LOAN — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
Invictus Bank
Loan Category: Personal Loan

================================================================================

Q1. What is the minimum and maximum loan amount for a personal loan?
A. The minimum personal loan amount is ₹50,000. The maximum is ₹25 lakh for
   salaried applicants and ₹15 lakh for self-employed applicants.

================================================================================

Q2. What is the interest rate on a personal loan?
A. Interest rates depend on your employment type and CIBIL score:
   - Salaried with CIBIL 750+: 11.5% per annum
   - Salaried with CIBIL 720-749: 13.0% per annum
   - Self-employed with CIBIL 750+: 13.5% per annum
   - Self-employed with CIBIL 720-749: 15.0% per annum
   Existing Invictus Bank salary account holders get an additional 0.5% concession.

================================================================================

Q3. What is the maximum repayment tenure?
A. The maximum repayment tenure for a personal loan is 60 months (5 years).
   The minimum tenure is 12 months.

================================================================================

Q4. Do I need to provide any collateral or security?
A. No. Personal loans from Invictus Bank are fully unsecured. No collateral,
   security, or mortgage is required. For loans up to ₹10 lakh, no guarantor
   is required either.

================================================================================

Q5. What is the minimum CIBIL score required?
A. The minimum CIBIL credit score required for a personal loan is 720.

================================================================================

Q6. What is the minimum salary required?
A. For salaried applicants, the minimum net monthly salary is ₹25,000 per month.
   For self-employed applicants, the minimum net annual income is ₹3 lakh per annum.

================================================================================

Q7. How long does it take to get the loan approved?
A. For salaried applicants with complete documentation, the loan is typically
   approved within 24 to 48 hours. Disbursement happens within 2 working days
   of approval.

================================================================================

Q8. Can I prepay my personal loan?
A. Pre-payment is not allowed within the first 6 months. After 6 months, you
   can make part pre-payments of up to 25% of the outstanding principal per year
   without any penalty. Full foreclosure is allowed after 12 months with a
   foreclosure charge of 3% on the outstanding principal.

================================================================================

Q9. What is the processing fee?
A. The processing fee is 2% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹1,500
   and a maximum of ₹15,000. GST is applicable on the processing fee.

================================================================================

Q10. Can I use a personal loan for any purpose?
A. Yes, personal loans can be used for any legitimate personal financial need
   including medical emergencies, travel, wedding expenses, home renovation,
   education fees, and debt consolidation. However, personal loans cannot be
   used for speculative investments (stock market, cryptocurrency) or for
   business purposes.

================================================================================

Q11. What documents are required for a personal loan?
A. For salaried applicants:
   - Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, or passport)
   - Address proof
   - Last 3 months salary slips
   - Form 16 for last 1 year
   - Bank statements for last 3 months
   - Employment ID card

   For self-employed applicants:
   - Identity proof and address proof
   - Last 2 years ITR with computation
   - Last 2 years audited financials
   - Bank statements for last 6 months
   - Business registration certificate

================================================================================

Q12. Is there an age limit for applying?
A. Yes. The minimum age is 21 years. The maximum age is 60 years for salaried
   applicants (at loan maturity) and 65 years for self-employed applicants
   (at loan maturity).

================================================================================

Q13. Can I apply jointly with another person?
A. Personal loans are typically issued to individual applicants. Joint applications
   are not available for personal loans. However, a guarantor may be required
   for loans above ₹10 lakh.

================================================================================

Q14. What happens if I miss an EMI?
A. A late payment charge of 2% per month on the overdue EMI amount will be
   levied. Consistent non-payment may negatively impact your CIBIL score and
   may result in legal action by the bank.

================================================================================
