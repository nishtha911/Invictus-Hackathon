VEHICLE LOAN — PRODUCT BROCHURE
Invictus Bank
"Drive Your Dreams"

================================================================================

About This Product

Invictus Bank's Vehicle Loan makes it easy to own the car or two-wheeler of
your dreams. With fast approvals, competitive rates, and financing up to 90%
of the vehicle's on-road price, we put you in the driver's seat.

================================================================================

Key Features

- New car loans up to ₹1 crore
- Financing up to 90% of on-road price for new cars
- Interest rates starting from 8.75% per annum for new cars
- Repayment tenure up to 84 months (7 years) for new cars
- Used car loans available for vehicles up to 5 years old
- Two-wheeler loans up to ₹5 lakh
- Quick approval within 24 hours for salaried applicants
- Doorstep document collection available

================================================================================

Products at a Glance

New Car Loan:
- Loan amount: ₹1 lakh to ₹1 crore
- Financing: Up to 90% of on-road price
- Interest rate: 8.75% to 9.5% p.a. (based on CIBIL score)
- Tenure: 12 to 84 months

Used Car Loan:
- Loan amount: ₹1 lakh to ₹25 lakh
- Financing: Up to 80% of assessed value
- Interest rate: 11.0% to 12.5% p.a. (based on CIBIL score)
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months
- Vehicle age: Not more than 5 years old

Two-Wheeler Loan:
- Loan amount: ₹20,000 to ₹5 lakh
- Financing: Up to 90% of on-road price
- Interest rate: 13.5% p.a.
- Tenure: 12 to 48 months

Commercial Vehicle Loan:
- For trucks, buses, and other commercial vehicles
- Interest rate: 10.5% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 60 months

================================================================================

Eligibility at a Glance

- Age: 21 to 65 years (at loan maturity)
- Minimum CIBIL score: 700
- Salaried: Minimum ₹20,000 per month net income
- Self-employed: Minimum ₹2.5 lakh per annum net income
- Farmers: Eligible with land ownership proof

================================================================================

Required Documents (Quick Reference)

Salaried:
- Identity and address proof
- Last 3 months salary slips
- Bank statements for last 3 months
- Vehicle proforma invoice

Self-Employed:
- Identity and address proof
- Last 2 years ITR
- Bank statements for last 6 months
- Vehicle proforma invoice

================================================================================

Processing Fee

- New car: 0.5% of loan amount (minimum ₹2,500)
- Used car: 1% of loan amount (minimum ₹3,000)
- Two-wheeler: Flat ₹1,500

================================================================================

How to Apply

1. Visit any Invictus Bank branch or apply online at www.invictusbank.com/vehicleloan
2. Submit documents and vehicle details
3. Loan approved within 24 hours for salaried applicants
4. Disbursement directly to the vehicle dealer
