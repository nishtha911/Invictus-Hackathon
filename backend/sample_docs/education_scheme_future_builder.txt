FutureBuilder Education Loan - Horizon Bank

Scheme Name: FutureBuilder Education Loan
Bank: Horizon Bank

Overview:
FutureBuilder supports undergraduate and postgraduate studies with flexible repayment options and partial loan disbursement directly to the educational institute.

Eligibility:
- Applicants aged 18-35.
- Parent or guardian may co-sign for applicants under 21.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,500,000
- Interest rate: 10.25% p.a. fixed for first 2 years, then floating
- Tenure: Up to 12 years
- Processing fee: Flat ₹2,500 or 0.75%

Special Conditions:
- Subsidised interest rates available for merit scholarship holders.
- Flexible EMI holiday options during internships if income proof is provided.

Documentation: Admission letter, fee invoice, ID and address proof, bank statements for guardian.
