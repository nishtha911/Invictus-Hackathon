Instant Gold Loan - Cognis Bank

Scheme Name: Instant Gold Loan
Bank: Cognis Bank

Overview:
Quick liquidity against physical gold ornaments with minimum documentation and zero hidden charges.

Eligibility:
- Any resident individual aged 18 to 70 years owning gold ornaments.
- No minimum income requirement.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹20,000 to ₹5,000,000
- Interest rate: From 7.90% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 12 months
- Processing fee: 0.50%

Special Conditions:
- Loan to Value (LTV) up to 75% of the appraised gold value.
- Repayment options include bullet repayment (principal + interest at maturity) or monthly interest servicing.

Documentation: Gold ownership self-declaration, ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN), and address proof.
