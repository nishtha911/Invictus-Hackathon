HOME LOAN — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
Invictus Bank
Loan Category: Home Loan

================================================================================

Q1. What is the maximum home loan amount I can get?
A. The maximum home loan amount is ₹5 crore. The minimum is ₹5 lakh.
   The actual loan amount depends on the property value, your income, and
   repayment capacity.

================================================================================

Q2. How much of the property value will the bank finance?
A. The Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio depends on the property value:
   - Properties up to ₹30 lakh: Up to 90% of property value
   - Properties between ₹30 lakh and ₹75 lakh: Up to 80% of property value
   - Properties above ₹75 lakh: Up to 75% of property value

================================================================================

Q3. What are the interest rates?
A. Floating rate home loans:
   - Loan up to ₹30 lakh: 8.5% per annum
   - Loan ₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh: 8.75% per annum
   - Loan above ₹75 lakh: 9.0% per annum
   Fixed rate (up to 10 years): 9.5% per annum
   Women applicants (primary) receive a concession of 0.05% on floating rates.

================================================================================

Q4. What is the maximum repayment tenure?
A. The maximum repayment tenure is 30 years. The minimum is 5 years.
   The tenure is subject to the applicant's age not exceeding 65 years at
   loan maturity.

================================================================================

Q5. What is the minimum CIBIL score required?
A. The minimum CIBIL score required for a home loan is 700.

================================================================================

Q6. What is the minimum income required?
A. For salaried applicants: Minimum ₹30,000 per month net income.
   For self-employed applicants: Minimum ₹4 lakh per annum net income.

================================================================================

Q7. What documents are required?
A. For salaried applicants:
   - Identity and address proof
   - Last 3 months salary slips
   - Form 16 for last 2 years
   - Bank statements for last 6 months
   - Property documents (sale agreement, title deed, approved plan, NOC)

   For self-employed applicants:
   - Identity and address proof
   - Last 3 years ITR with computation
   - Last 3 years audited financials
   - Bank statements for last 6 months
   - Property documents

================================================================================

Q8. What is the processing fee?
A. Processing fee is 0.5% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹5,000
   and a maximum of ₹25,000. Legal and technical charges are additional.

================================================================================

Q9. Can I prepay my home loan?
A. For floating rate loans: Part pre-payment and full foreclosure are allowed
   without any penalty after 12 months.
   For fixed rate loans: Foreclosure charge of 2% on outstanding principal applies.

================================================================================

Q10. What are the tax benefits on a home loan?
A. - Principal repayment: Deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh per year under Section 80C
   - Interest paid: Deduction up to ₹2 lakh per year under Section 24(b)
   - First-time buyers: Additional ₹50,000 deduction under Section 80EE

================================================================================

Q11. Is insurance mandatory?
A. Yes. The property must be insured against fire and natural calamities for
   the full loan tenure. The cost of insurance is borne by the borrower.

================================================================================

Q12. Can I get a home loan for an under-construction property?
A. Yes. For under-construction properties, the loan is disbursed in stages
   based on construction progress. Pre-EMI interest is charged on the disbursed
   amount until full disbursement, after which regular EMIs begin.

================================================================================
