SmartAuto Loan - DriveBank

Scheme Name: SmartAuto Loan
Bank: DriveBank

Overview:
Financing for new and used cars with competitive EMI plans and dealer tie-ups for quick processing.

Eligibility:
- Salaried applicants with stable employment.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹5,000,000
- Interest rate: From 8.5% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 7 years
- Processing fee: 0.5%

Special Conditions:
- Dealer-assist disbursal available; insurance included as add-on.

Documentation: Vehicle invoice, ID, income proof.
