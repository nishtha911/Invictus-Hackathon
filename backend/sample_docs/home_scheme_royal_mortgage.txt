Royal Mortgage - Heritage Bank

Scheme Name: Royal Mortgage
Bank: Heritage Bank

Overview:
Full-featured home loan for salaried and self-employed applicants with competitive interest rates and long tenures.

Eligibility:
- Age 21-65 for primary applicant.
- Minimum monthly income: ₹30,000 for salaried applicants.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹500,000 to ₹100,000,000
- Interest rate: From 7.25% p.a. (floating)
- Tenure: Up to 30 years
- Processing fee: 0.5% of sanctioned amount

Special Conditions:
- Part prepayment allowed with no penalty after 12 months.
- LTV and FOIR checks applied per policy.

Documentation: Property documents, title search, income proof, bank statements.
