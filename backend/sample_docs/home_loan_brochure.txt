================================================================================

About This Product

Invictus Bank's Home Loan makes owning your dream home a reality. With attractive
interest rates, flexible repayment options, and minimal documentation, we make
the home buying process simple and stress-free.

================================================================================

Key Features

- Loan amount from ₹5 lakh up to ₹5 crore
- Interest rates starting from 8.5% per annum
- Repayment tenure up to 30 years
- Up to 90% financing of property value for properties up to ₹30 lakh
- No pre-payment penalty on floating rate loans
- Special concession of 0.05% for women applicants
- Balance transfer facility available from other banks

================================================================================

Purpose of Loan

- Purchase of a new residential flat or house
- Purchase of a resale residential property
- Construction of a house on owned plot
- Home extension or renovation (up to ₹50 lakh)
- Plot purchase + construction (combined loan)

================================================================================

Loan Amount and LTV

Property Value                     Maximum LTV
Up to ₹30 lakh                     90% of property value
₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh              80% of property value
Above ₹75 lakh                     75% of property value

================================================================================

Interest Rates at a Glance

Loan Amount                        Floating Rate (p.a.)
Up to ₹30 lakh                     8.5%
₹30 lakh to ₹75 lakh              8.75%
Above ₹75 lakh                     9.0%
Fixed rate (up to 10 years)        9.5%
Women applicant concession         0.05% reduction

================================================================================

Eligibility at a Glance

- Age: 21 to 65 years (at loan maturity)
- Minimum CIBIL score: 700
- Salaried: Minimum ₹30,000 per month income
- Self-employed: Minimum ₹4 lakh per annum income
- Minimum employment: 2 years (salaried), 3 years (self-employed)

================================================================================

Tax Benefits

- Principal repayment: Deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh per year under Section 80C
- Interest paid: Deduction up to ₹2 lakh per year under Section 24(b)
- First-time home buyers: Additional deduction of ₹50,000 under Section 80EE
  (subject to conditions)

================================================================================

Balance Transfer

Existing home loan borrowers from other banks can transfer their outstanding
loan to Invictus Bank and benefit from:
- Lower interest rates
- Top-up loan facility up to ₹50 lakh
- No processing fee on balance transfer (limited period offer)

================================================================================

How to Apply

1. Visit any Invictus Bank branch or apply online at www.invictusbank.com/homeloan
2. Submit documents and property details
3. Technical and legal verification of property
4. Loan sanctioned within 5 working days of complete documentation
5. Disbursement within 3 working days of sanction
