PERSONAL LOAN POLICY
Invictus Bank — Retail Lending Division
Document Type: Policy
Loan Category: Personal Loan
Version: 2.3
Effective Date: 01-April-2024

================================================================================

Overview

Invictus Bank offers personal loans to salaried and self-employed individuals
for meeting personal financial needs including medical emergencies, travel,
wedding expenses, home renovation, and other personal requirements.
Personal loans are unsecured — no collateral is required.

================================================================================

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be an Indian resident citizen.
2. Minimum age: 21 years.
3. Maximum age: 60 years for salaried applicants at loan maturity.
4. Maximum age: 65 years for self-employed applicants at loan maturity.
5. Salaried applicants:
   - Minimum monthly net salary: ₹25,000 per month.
   - Minimum 1 year of continuous employment with current employer.
   - Minimum total work experience: 2 years.
6. Self-employed applicants:
   - Minimum annual net income: ₹3 lakh per annum.
   - Minimum 2 years of business continuity.
7. Minimum CIBIL credit score: 720.
8. Existing Invictus Bank customers with a good repayment track record may
   receive preferential processing.

================================================================================

Loan Amount

1. Minimum loan amount: ₹50,000.
2. Maximum loan amount for salaried applicants: ₹25 lakh.
3. Maximum loan amount for self-employed applicants: ₹15 lakh.
4. Loan amount is subject to a maximum of 20 times the net monthly salary
   for salaried applicants.
5. Maximum EMI-to-income ratio: 50% of net monthly income.

================================================================================

Interest Rate

1. Salaried applicants:
   - CIBIL score 750 and above: 11.5% per annum.
   - CIBIL score 720 to 749: 13.0% per annum.
2. Self-employed applicants:
   - CIBIL score 750 and above: 13.5% per annum.
   - CIBIL score 720 to 749: 15.0% per annum.
3. Interest rates are fixed for the entire loan tenure.
4. Existing Invictus Bank salary account holders receive a concession of
   0.5% on the applicable rate.

================================================================================

Tenure

1. Minimum tenure: 12 months.
2. Maximum tenure: 60 months (5 years).

================================================================================

Collateral

Personal loans are fully unsecured. No collateral or security is required.
No guarantor is required for loans up to ₹10 lakh.
For loans above ₹10 lakh, a guarantor with a stable income may be required
at the bank's discretion.

================================================================================

Required Documents

For Salaried Applicants:
1. Completed loan application form with photographs
2. Identity proof: Aadhaar card, PAN card, or passport
3. Address proof: Utility bill, Aadhaar card, or bank statement
4. Last 3 months salary slips
5. Form 16 for last 1 year
6. Bank statements for last 3 months showing salary credits
7. Employment ID card or offer letter

For Self-Employed Applicants:
1. Completed loan application form with photographs
2. Identity proof and address proof
3. Last 2 years Income Tax Returns with computation
4. Last 2 years audited financial statements
5. Bank statements for last 6 months
6. Business registration certificate or GST registration

================================================================================

Processing Fee

1. Processing fee: 2% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹1,500
   and a maximum of ₹15,000.
2. Processing fee is non-refundable after loan disbursement.
3. GST applicable on processing fee as per prevailing rates.

================================================================================

Repayment

1. Repayment is through Equated Monthly Instalments (EMI).
2. First EMI begins 30 days after disbursement.
3. Pre-payment is not allowed within the first 6 months of disbursement.
4. After 6 months, part pre-payment is allowed up to 25% of outstanding
   principal per year without penalty.
5. Foreclosure (full pre-payment) is allowed after 12 months with a
   foreclosure charge of 3% on the outstanding principal.
6. Repayment through auto-debit from Invictus Bank account is mandatory.

================================================================================

Special Conditions

1. The loan amount is disbursed directly to the applicant's Invictus Bank
   savings account.
2. The bank reserves the right to verify the end use of the loan.
3. Any misrepresentation of purpose may result in immediate recall of the loan.
4. The bank may offer pre-approved personal loans to existing customers based
   on their banking relationship and credit history.

================================================================================

Restrictions

1. Personal loans cannot be used for speculative purposes including stock
   market investments or cryptocurrency.
2. Loans cannot be used for business purposes — a separate business loan
   product is available.
3. Personal loans are not available to applicants who are already in default
   on any existing loan with any bank or financial institution.

================================================================================
