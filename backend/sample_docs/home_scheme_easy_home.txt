EasyHome Loan - Neighbourhood Bank

Scheme Name: EasyHome Loan
Bank: Neighbourhood Bank

Overview:
Affordable home loan product with streamlined documentation for first-time home buyers.

Eligibility:
- Salaried applicants with a minimum 2 years employment.
- Minimum monthly income: ₹20,000.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹250,000 to ₹30,000,000
- Interest rate: From 8.10% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 25 years
- Processing fee: 0.75%

Special Conditions:
- Reduced processing fee for salaried applicants with employer tie-ups.
- Interest subsidy schemes may apply subject to government policy.

Documentation: Salary slips, bank statements, property valuation certificate.
