FlexiMortgage - Metropolitan Bank

Scheme Name: FlexiMortgage
Bank: Metropolitan Bank

Overview:
Flexible mortgage with overdraft-style features and interest-only payment options for a defined period.

Eligibility:
- Salaried and business owners with steady income.
- Minimum income threshold assessed case-by-case.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹1,000,000 to ₹200,000,000
- Interest rate: From 7.75% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 30 years
- Processing fee: 0.9%

Special Conditions:
- Interest-only option for the first 2 years available on request.
- Requires property insurance.

Documentation: Proof of income, business statements for self-employed, property title.
