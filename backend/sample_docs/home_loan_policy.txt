HOME LOAN POLICY
Invictus Bank — Retail Lending Division
Document Type: Policy
Loan Category: Home Loan
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 01-April-2024

================================================================================

Overview

Invictus Bank provides home loans to eligible individuals and families for the
purpose of purchasing, constructing, or renovating residential properties.
The bank offers competitive interest rates and flexible repayment options.

================================================================================

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be an Indian resident citizen or NRI (Non-Resident Indian).
2. Minimum age: 21 years at the time of loan application.
3. Maximum age: 65 years at the time of loan maturity.
4. Salaried applicants must have a minimum of 2 years of continuous employment.
5. Self-employed applicants must have a minimum of 3 years of business continuity.
6. Minimum monthly income for salaried applicants: ₹30,000 per month.
7. Minimum annual income for self-employed applicants: ₹4 lakh per annum.
8. Minimum CIBIL credit score: 700.
9. Joint applications are permitted. Co-applicant's income can be clubbed.

================================================================================

Loan Amount

1. Minimum loan amount: ₹5 lakh.
2. Maximum loan amount: ₹5 crore.
3. Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio:
   - For properties up to ₹30 lakh: Up to 90% of property value.
   - For properties between ₹30 lakh and ₹75 lakh: Up to 80% of property value.
   - For properties above ₹75 lakh: Up to 75% of property value.
4. The loan amount is subject to the applicant's repayment capacity.
5. Maximum EMI-to-income ratio: 50% of net monthly income.

================================================================================

Interest Rate

1. Floating rate home loans:
   - For loan amounts up to ₹30 lakh: 8.5% per annum.
   - For loan amounts above ₹30 lakh and up to ₹75 lakh: 8.75% per annum.
   - For loan amounts above ₹75 lakh: 9.0% per annum.
2. Fixed rate home loans:
   - Available for tenures up to 10 years at 9.5% per annum.
3. Women applicants (primary applicant) receive a concession of 0.05% on
   the applicable floating rate.
4. Interest rates are linked to the bank's Repo Rate Linked Lending Rate (RLLR).

================================================================================

Tenure

1. Minimum tenure: 5 years.
2. Maximum tenure: 30 years.
3. Tenure is subject to the applicant's age at maturity not exceeding 65 years.

================================================================================

Collateral

1. The property being purchased or constructed serves as the primary security.
2. The bank will hold the original title documents of the property until the
   loan is fully repaid.
3. Additional collateral may be required if the property value is insufficient.
4. The property must be free from all encumbrances.
5. The property must be insured against fire and natural calamities for the
   full loan tenure at the borrower's cost.

================================================================================

Required Documents

For Salaried Applicants:
1. Completed loan application form with photographs
2. Identity proof: Aadhaar card, PAN card, or passport
3. Address proof: Utility bill, Aadhaar card, or bank statement
4. Last 3 months salary slips
5. Form 16 for last 2 years
6. Bank statements for last 6 months
7. Property documents:
   - Sale agreement / allotment letter
   - Title deed / chain of title documents
   - Approved building plan
   - NOC from builder/society

For Self-Employed Applicants:
1. All documents listed above (except salary slips and Form 16)
2. Last 3 years Income Tax Returns with computation
3. Last 3 years audited financial statements (P&L and Balance Sheet)
4. Business registration certificate
5. GST registration (if applicable)

================================================================================

Processing Fee

1. Processing fee: 0.5% of the loan amount, subject to a minimum of ₹5,000
   and a maximum of ₹25,000.
2. Processing fee is non-refundable after loan sanction.
3. Legal and technical charges are additional and payable by the applicant.

================================================================================

Repayment

1. Repayment is through Equated Monthly Instalments (EMI).
2. EMI begins from the month following full disbursement.
3. For under-construction properties, Pre-EMI interest is charged on the
   disbursed amount until full disbursement.
4. Part pre-payment is allowed without penalty for floating rate loans.
5. Full pre-payment (foreclosure) is allowed without penalty for floating
   rate loans after 12 months.
6. For fixed rate loans, a foreclosure charge of 2% on the outstanding
   principal applies.

================================================================================

Special Conditions

1. The property must be located within India.
2. The bank will conduct a technical and legal verification of the property
   before sanction.
3. The applicant must obtain all necessary approvals and NOCs before disbursement.
4. The loan is disbursed in stages for under-construction properties based on
   construction progress.
5. The borrower must maintain a savings account with Invictus Bank for EMI
   auto-debit.

================================================================================

Restrictions

1. Home loans are not available for commercial properties.
2. Loans are not available for properties with disputed titles.
3. Agricultural land cannot be used as collateral.
4. The loan cannot be used for purchasing plots unless construction begins
   within 2 years of plot purchase.

================================================================================
