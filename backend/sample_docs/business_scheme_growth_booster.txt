GrowthBooster Business Loan - SME Bank

Scheme Name: GrowthBooster Business Loan
Bank: SME Bank

Overview:
Working capital and term loan facility for small and medium enterprises to support expansion.

Eligibility:
- Registered businesses with minimum 2 years of operations.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹200,000 to ₹20,000,000
- Interest rate: From 12.0% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 7 years
- Processing fee: 1.5%

Special Conditions:
- Collateral/guarantee requirements depend on loan size and credit profile.

Documentation: Business registration, financial statements, bank statements.
