ScholarPlus Education Loan - BrightFutures Bank

Scheme Name: ScholarPlus Education Loan
Bank: BrightFutures Bank

Overview:
ScholarPlus provides funding for higher education, vocational training and study-abroad programs. The scheme supports tuition, accommodation and approved course-related expenses.

Eligibility:
- Indian resident students or guardians applying on behalf of a student.
- Minimum age: 18 years (applicant/guardian criteria vary).
- Academic admission proof (offer letter or fee invoice) required.

Financials:
- Minimum monthly family income: No minimum for co-signed loans; income used to determine interest band.
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹2,000,000
- Interest rate: From 9.5% p.a. (floating) depending on credit assessment
- Tenure: Up to 10 years
- Processing fee: 1.0% of sanctioned amount

Special Conditions:
- Moratorium available until course completion + 6 months grace.
- Collateral required for loans above ₹1,000,000 or if no viable guarantor.

Documentation: Offer letter, fee schedule, ID, address, academic records, guarantor documents.
