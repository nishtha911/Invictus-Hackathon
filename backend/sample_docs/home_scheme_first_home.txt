FirstHome Advantage - Community Lend

Scheme Name: FirstHome Advantage
Bank: Community Lend

Overview:
Targeted at first-time home buyers with reduced fees and advisory support for paperwork.

Eligibility:
- First-time buyer declaration required.
- Minimum monthly income: ₹18,000.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹200,000 to ₹20,000,000
- Interest rate: From 8.5% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 20 years
- Processing fee: 0.4% for first-time buyers

Special Conditions:
- Pre-approved educational counselling and lower documentation thresholds.

Documentation: ID, proof of first-time buyer status, income proofs, property documents.
