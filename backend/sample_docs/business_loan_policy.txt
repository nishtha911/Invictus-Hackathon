BUSINESS LOAN POLICY
Invictus Bank — SME and Business Banking Division
Document Type: Policy
Loan Category: Business Loan
Version: 1.8
Effective Date: 01-April-2024

================================================================================

Overview

Invictus Bank offers business loans to small and medium enterprises (SMEs),
self-employed professionals, and business owners to meet working capital needs,
business expansion, equipment purchase, and other business-related requirements.

================================================================================

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be an Indian resident.
2. Eligible entities:
   - Sole proprietorships
   - Partnership firms
   - Private limited companies
   - Limited liability partnerships (LLPs)
   - Self-employed professionals (doctors, CAs, architects, etc.)
3. Minimum age of proprietor/partner/director: 25 years.
4. Maximum age of proprietor/partner/director: 65 years at loan maturity.
5. Minimum business vintage: 3 years of continuous operation.
6. Minimum annual business turnover: ₹10 lakh per annum.
7. Minimum net annual profit: ₹2 lakh per annum (as per ITR).
8. Minimum CIBIL score: 700 (for proprietor/partners/directors).
9. The business must not have any existing NPA (Non-Performing Asset) with
   any bank or financial institution.

================================================================================

Loan Amount

Unsecured Business Loans:
1. Minimum loan amount: ₹1 lakh.
2. Maximum loan amount: ₹50 lakh.
3. Loan amount is subject to a maximum of 3 times the annual net profit.

Secured Business Loans:
1. Minimum loan amount: ₹5 lakh.
2. Maximum loan amount: ₹5 crore.
3. Loan amount is subject to collateral value and repayment capacity.

================================================================================

Interest Rate

Unsecured Business Loans:
- CIBIL score 750 and above: 14.0% per annum.
- CIBIL score 700 to 749: 16.0% per annum.

Secured Business Loans:
- CIBIL score 750 and above: 11.0% per annum.
- CIBIL score 700 to 749: 12.5% per annum.

Working Capital Loans (Overdraft/CC):
- Standard rate: 13.5% per annum on utilized amount.

Equipment Finance Loans:
- Standard rate: 10.5% per annum.

================================================================================

Tenure

Unsecured Business Loans:
- Minimum tenure: 12 months.
- Maximum tenure: 48 months (4 years).

Secured Business Loans (Term Loans):
- Minimum tenure: 12 months.
- Maximum tenure: 120 months (10 years).

Working Capital (Overdraft/CC):
- Renewed annually subject to satisfactory conduct.

================================================================================

Collateral

Unsecured Business Loans (up to ₹50 lakh):
- No collateral required.
- Personal guarantee of proprietor/all partners/directors is mandatory.

Secured Business Loans (above ₹50 lakh):
- Immovable property (commercial or residential) as primary security.
- Minimum collateral coverage: 125% of loan amount.
- Additional collateral may include:
  - Fixed deposits
  - Machinery and equipment (for equipment loans)
  - Stock and book debts (for working capital loans)

================================================================================

Required Documents

1. Completed loan application form with photographs
2. Identity proof of proprietor/partners/directors
3. Address proof of business and proprietor/partners/directors
4. Business registration documents:
   - Sole proprietorship: GST registration / Shop Act license
   - Partnership: Partnership deed
   - Private limited: Certificate of incorporation, MOA, AOA
   - LLP: LLP agreement and certificate of incorporation
5. Last 3 years Income Tax Returns (business and personal)
6. Last 3 years audited financial statements (P&L and Balance Sheet)
7. Bank statements for last 12 months (all business accounts)
8. GST returns for last 12 months
9. List of existing loans with repayment track record
10. Collateral documents (if applicable)
11. Project report (for new projects or expansion loans)

================================================================================

Processing Fee

1. Unsecured business loans: 2% of the loan amount, minimum ₹2,000.
2. Secured business loans: 1% of the loan amount, minimum ₹5,000,
   maximum ₹50,000.
3. Working capital facilities: 1% of the sanctioned limit per annum.
4. Processing fee is non-refundable after loan sanction.

================================================================================

Repayment

1. Term loans: Repayment through monthly EMIs.
2. Working capital (OD/CC): Interest payable monthly on utilized amount;
   principal repayable on demand or at renewal.
3. Pre-payment of term loans is allowed after 12 months with a pre-payment
   charge of 2% on the outstanding principal.
4. Foreclosure is allowed after 24 months without penalty for secured loans.

================================================================================

Special Conditions

1. The bank may conduct periodic inspections of the business premises and
   review of financial statements.
2. The borrower must maintain a current account with Invictus Bank for
   routing business transactions.
3. Any change in business ownership, constitution, or key management must
   be immediately informed to the bank.
4. The bank reserves the right to convert the loan to a secured loan if the
   business performance deteriorates.

================================================================================

Restrictions

1. Business loans cannot be used for personal expenses.
2. Loans are not available for businesses involved in speculative activities.
3. Loans are not available for businesses with negative net worth.
4. Agricultural businesses must apply under the separate Agricultural Loan scheme.

================================================================================
