EasyDrive Auto Loan - RoadTrust Bank

Scheme Name: EasyDrive Auto Loan
Bank: RoadTrust Bank

Overview:
Affordable auto loan for first-time buyers with flexible down-payment options.

Eligibility:
- Minimum driver age 21; salaried or self-employed applicants.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹40,000 to ₹3,000,000
- Interest rate: From 9.0% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 6 years
- Processing fee: 0.6%

Special Conditions:
- Low down-payment schemes tied to employer partnerships.

Documentation: Vehicle invoice, KYC, income proof.
