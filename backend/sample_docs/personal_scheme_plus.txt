PersonalPlus - TrustLend

Scheme Name: PersonalPlus
Bank: TrustLend

Overview:
Balanced personal loan offering with partial pre-approval and flexible tenure.

Eligibility:
- Applicants aged 21-60 with stable income.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹30,000 to ₹1,500,000
- Interest rate: From 11.0% p.a.
- Tenure: 1 to 7 years
- Processing fee: 1.0%

Special Conditions:
- Pre-approved customers may receive lower rates.

Documentation: ID, income proof, bank statement.
