SkillBoost Short-term Loan - CareerAid Bank

Scheme Name: SkillBoost Short-term Loan
Bank: CareerAid Bank

Overview:
Short-term financing for professional certifications, bootcamps and skill-based courses. Quick approval and simple documentation.

Eligibility:
- Applicants aged 18 and above.
- Minimum demonstrable intent (course invoice) required.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹10,000 to ₹200,000
- Interest rate: 12.5% p.a. (fixed)
- Tenure: 1 to 3 years
- Processing fee: ₹999 flat

Special Conditions:
- Fast-track disbursal within 48 hours for partnered institutes.
- No collateral.

Documentation: Fee invoice, ID, and minimal income proof (if available).
