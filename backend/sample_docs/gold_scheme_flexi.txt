Flexi Gold Overdraft - Cognis Bank

Scheme Name: Flexi Gold Overdraft
Bank: Cognis Bank

Overview:
Overdraft facility backed by physical gold ornaments, allowing you to withdraw funds as needed and pay interest only on the utilized amount.

Eligibility:
- Salaried, self-employed, or business owners aged 21 to 65 years.
- Gold ornaments must be 18 to 22 karats.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹50,000 to ₹10,000,000
- Interest rate: From 8.50% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 24 months (renewables available)
- Processing fee: 1.00%

Special Conditions:
- Prepayment and foreclosure allowed at zero extra charges.
- Interest calculated daily and debited monthly based on actual utilization.

Documentation: ID proof, address proof, gold ornaments testing report (conducted in-branch).
