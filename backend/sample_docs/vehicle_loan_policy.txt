VEHICLE LOAN POLICY
Invictus Bank — Retail Lending Division
Document Type: Policy
Loan Category: Vehicle Loan
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 01-April-2024

================================================================================

Overview

Invictus Bank provides vehicle loans for the purchase of new and used cars,
two-wheelers, and commercial vehicles. The bank offers competitive interest
rates with quick processing and minimal documentation.

================================================================================

Eligibility

1. Applicant must be an Indian resident citizen.
2. Minimum age: 21 years.
3. Maximum age: 65 years at loan maturity.
4. Salaried applicants:
   - Minimum monthly net income: ₹20,000 per month.
   - Minimum 1 year of continuous employment.
5. Self-employed applicants:
   - Minimum annual net income: ₹2.5 lakh per annum.
   - Minimum 2 years of business continuity.
6. Minimum CIBIL credit score: 700.
7. Farmers and agriculturists are eligible with proof of land ownership and
   income from agriculture.

================================================================================

Loan Amount

New Car Loans:
1. Minimum loan amount: ₹1 lakh.
2. Maximum loan amount: ₹1 crore.
3. Maximum financing: Up to 90% of the on-road price of the vehicle.

Used Car Loans:
1. Minimum loan amount: ₹1 lakh.
2. Maximum loan amount: ₹25 lakh.
3. Maximum financing: Up to 80% of the assessed value of the vehicle.
4. Vehicle must not be more than 5 years old at the time of loan application.
5. Vehicle must not be more than 10 years old at the time of loan maturity.

Two-Wheeler Loans:
1. Minimum loan amount: ₹20,000.
2. Maximum loan amount: ₹5 lakh.
3. Maximum financing: Up to 90% of the on-road price.

================================================================================

Interest Rate

New Car Loans:
- CIBIL score 750 and above: 8.75% per annum (floating).
- CIBIL score 700 to 749: 9.5% per annum (floating).

Used Car Loans:
- CIBIL score 750 and above: 11.0% per annum (floating).
- CIBIL score 700 to 749: 12.5% per annum (floating).

Two-Wheeler Loans:
- Standard rate: 13.5% per annum (floating).

Commercial Vehicle Loans:
- Standard rate: 10.5% per annum (floating).

================================================================================

Tenure

New Car Loans:
- Minimum tenure: 12 months.
- Maximum tenure: 84 months (7 years).

Used Car Loans:
- Minimum tenure: 12 months.
- Maximum tenure: 60 months (5 years).

Two-Wheeler Loans:
- Minimum tenure: 12 months.
- Maximum tenure: 48 months (4 years).

================================================================================

Collateral

1. The vehicle being purchased serves as the primary security (hypothecation).
2. The bank's name will be endorsed on the Registration Certificate (RC) as
   hypothecatee until the loan is fully repaid.
3. Comprehensive insurance of the vehicle is mandatory throughout the loan tenure.
4. The bank must be named as the beneficiary in the insurance policy.

================================================================================

Required Documents

For Salaried Applicants:
1. Completed loan application form with photographs
2. Identity proof: Aadhaar card, PAN card, or passport
3. Address proof: Utility bill, Aadhaar card, or bank statement
4. Last 3 months salary slips
5. Bank statements for last 3 months
6. Vehicle proforma invoice (for new vehicles)
7. RC book and insurance copy (for used vehicles)
8. Valuation report from bank-approved valuer (for used vehicles)

For Self-Employed Applicants:
1. All documents listed above (except salary slips)
2. Last 2 years Income Tax Returns
3. Bank statements for last 6 months
4. Business registration certificate

================================================================================

Processing Fee

1. New car loans: 0.5% of the loan amount, minimum ₹2,500.
2. Used car loans: 1% of the loan amount, minimum ₹3,000.
3. Two-wheeler loans: Flat fee of ₹1,500.
4. Processing fee is non-refundable after loan sanction.

================================================================================

Repayment

1. Repayment is through Equated Monthly Instalments (EMI).
2. First EMI begins 30 days after disbursement.
3. Pre-payment is allowed after 6 months without penalty for floating rate loans.
4. Foreclosure charge of 2% on outstanding principal applies within the first
   6 months.
5. The loan is disbursed directly to the vehicle dealer.

================================================================================

Special Conditions

1. The vehicle must be registered in the applicant's name within 30 days of
   purchase.
2. The applicant must submit the original RC book to the bank within 60 days
   of vehicle registration.
3. The bank reserves the right to inspect the vehicle at any time during the
   loan tenure.
4. In case of vehicle theft or total loss, the insurance claim proceeds will
   be used to settle the outstanding loan amount.

================================================================================

Restrictions

1. Loans are not available for vehicles registered outside India.
2. Used vehicles with more than 5 years of age are not eligible.
3. Loans are not available for vehicles purchased at auction.
4. Racing or modified vehicles are not eligible for financing.

================================================================================
