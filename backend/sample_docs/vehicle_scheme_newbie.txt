NewbieCar Starter Loan - AutoStart Bank

Scheme Name: NewbieCar Starter Loan
Bank: AutoStart Bank

Overview:
Small-ticket auto loan for recent graduates and first-time car buyers.

Eligibility:
- Applicants aged 21-30 with proof of employment or co-signer.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹30,000 to ₹500,000
- Interest rate: From 10.0% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 5 years
- Processing fee: ₹1,000 flat

Special Conditions:
- Co-signer accepted; simplified underwriting for salaried applicants.

Documentation: ID, job offer/appointment letter or co-signer documents.
