SMEPlus Loan - CapitalWorks Bank

Scheme Name: SMEPlus Loan
Bank: CapitalWorks Bank

Overview:
Tailored term loans for SMEs focused on capex and machinery purchases.

Eligibility:
- Businesses with audited accounts or verifiable revenue.

Financials:
- Loan amount: ₹500,000 to ₹50,000,000
- Interest rate: From 11.5% p.a.
- Tenure: Up to 10 years
- Processing fee: 1.25%

Special Conditions:
- Asset-backed lending options and structured repayment plans.

Documentation: Financial statements, asset invoices, business proof.
